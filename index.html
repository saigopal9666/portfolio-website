To Every Professional Living Away From Parents: How I Built an AI Voice Agent for My Parents "Jiddu" AI

An empathetic, autonomous Voice AI companion built for parents living away from home. Automatically places scheduled calls in native Telugu, tracks daily medications & health vitals, and logs instant reports with family alerts. Here is how the architecture works:

  • Vapi: Initiates outbound calls on schedule (Morning/Evening).
  • Azure STT (Ears): Listens and transcribes the Telugu speech.
  • GPT-4 (Brain): Asks about specific tablets by name and generates contextual replies.
  • OpenAI TTS (Voice): Speaks back in a natural, respectful Telugu tone.
  • Make.com + Notion: Parses responses, logs them into a database, and sends instant alerts.
